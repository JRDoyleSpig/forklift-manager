Help & Training
How the Forklift Manager works, when to use each workflow, and what data is saved where.
The most important rule: what is the master data?
Forklift Transactions is the financial master record. If money was spent on a forklift, the cost should ultimately exist in Transactions. That is what drives yearly expense, lifetime expense, repair expense, PM spend, rental spend, service-call counts, and Aging / Grading cost metrics.

The other SharePoint lists have different jobs: Forklift Fleet is the current-state master for the asset; Forklift Service Events records the operational story of a PM or repair visit and holds its work orders/attachments; Forklift Hour Readings is the meter-history master; and Forklift Rentals tracks open rentals and costs that may not be known yet. These lists work together rather than replacing Transactions.

Do I need to Log PM or Log Repair every time?

For a complete operational history, use Log PM or Log Repair whenever an actual service event occurs and you have useful service information to preserve. That is especially valuable when you have a work order, technician notes, an hour reading, downtime, a rental, or status information.

You do not need to create a fake service event just because an accounting transaction exists. If all you have is the monthly NetSuite expense, importing the transaction is enough for the financial record. Likewise, a work order with hours but no cost can be saved as service/hour history without inventing an expense.

The practical rule is: Transactions answer “what did we spend?” Service Events answer “what happened to the forklift?” When you have both pieces of information, both records are useful and are linked by Service Event ID.

Very important — avoiding duplicate expenses

If you enter known expense lines while logging a PM or repair, those lines are written into Forklift Transactions immediately. If the same invoice later appears in a NetSuite CSV, the import duplicate check should identify matching Date + Forklift ID + Invoice Number + Amount lines and mark them to Skip.

If the invoice amount is not known yet, leave the expense amount blank when logging the service event. Save the operational event, hours, documents, downtime, and rental information now; let the later NetSuite import become the financial transaction when the invoice posts.

Always review the NetSuite preview before importing. A changed date, invoice number, forklift assignment, or split amount can prevent an otherwise related line from matching as a duplicate.

Fleet — adding and editing forklifts

The Fleet list represents the forklift’s current state: ID, make/model/serial, current workroom, status, current hours, primary service vendor, PM frequency, attachments/poles, acquisition information, and notes.

Double-click a forklift to edit it. Current workrooms are validated against active Lease Manager locations for Active, Down for Repair, and Parked units. Retired/Scrapped/Sold units may remain associated with an inactive historical location.

Changing Current Hours automatically creates a reading in Forklift Hour Readings. If the new reading is lower than the current reading, the app warns you and requires confirmation.

Status definitions

Active: In normal operating service.

Down for Repair: Temporarily unavailable because it needs or is receiving repair. It is expected to return to service.

Parked: Still owned and physically retained, but intentionally not being used or repaired at this time. It may return to service later. Parked units remain visible but are excluded from the Active count.

Retired: Permanently removed from normal operating service with no intention to return it to regular use. It may still be physically on site while disposition is decided.

Sold: Ownership has been transferred through sale.

Scrapped: Permanently disposed of as scrap.

Disposition Date is manually entered for Retired, Sold, and Scrapped. The app does not automatically invent a disposition date.

Transactions — financial master data

Transactions contains the expense lines used for financial reporting and grading. Negative transactions/credits reduce totals. The app calculates yearly expense and lifetime expense live from the transaction history rather than storing fixed totals on the forklift.

Typical categories include PM Service, Repair / Expense, Rental, and other expense classifications. PM expense counts toward total operating expense but is excluded from Repair Expense YTD used in the Aging / Grading repair score. Rental expense also counts toward total expense but is currently kept separate from grading repair cost.

Double-click a transaction to correct fields such as forklift, workroom, vendor, invoice, amount, category, service-call flag, hours, downtime, rental required, description, URL, or notes.

NetSuite CSV import

Use the monthly NetSuite CSV for ongoing accounting expenses. The importer reads the forklift from the Memo, vendor from Main Line Name, and presents a review screen before anything is saved.

PMs are suggested using the known PM patterns (including the common $135 PM labor charge), but the category remains editable. Repair invoices can receive one high-confidence Service Call suggestion. PMs never count as service calls.

Primary Service Vendor is compared during review. A different vendor does not automatically replace the primary vendor because one-off vendors are common.

What exactly counts as a Service Call?

A service call represents the visit/event, not every invoice line. A multi-line repair invoice may contain labor, parts, freight, and other charges, but only one line should carry Service Call = Yes for that repair event.

PMs always have Service Call = No. Rental-only expense lines also do not count as service calls. This prevents service-call counts from being inflated by accounting line detail.

Log PM — when and how to use it

Use Log PM when a planned/preventive maintenance visit actually occurs and you want to preserve the operational record. Select the forklift and service date; verify the vendor; enter the work order/invoice reference; add the hour reading if known; attach the work order/invoice; enter work performed/notes; and add expense lines only if the cost is already known.

Saving a PM creates a Service Event. Any entered expense lines become PM Service Transactions. PM lines never count as service calls and are excluded from repair-cost grading. If the hours changed, Current Hours and Hour Readings are updated. Last PM Date is updated, and PM Frequency can also be maintained from the form.

If you have the PM work order today but will not know the invoice amount until accounting receives it, save the PM event without an expense amount. The later NetSuite CSV can supply the financial transaction.

Log Repair — when and how to use it

Use Log Repair when a repair/service visit occurs and you want more than just the eventual accounting charge. Record the service date, vendor, work order/invoice, hours, repair description, downtime, attachments, rental requirement, and optional status change.

Expense lines can be split into Labor, Parts, Freight, Rental, and Other. They remain grouped under one Service Event. The first non-rental repair expense line is the one counted as the service call; the remaining lines do not inflate the count.

If repair cost is unknown, leave the expense line amounts blank. The service event can still exist now, and the later invoice/NetSuite import can create the financial transaction.

Work Order / Hours upload

This workflow is for work orders or service documents that may contain an hour-meter reading, even when there is no invoice. The app reads text from the uploaded PDF and looks for serial number, Hours, Work Order number, Date Completed (preferred), Date Started, vendor indicators, and PM wording.

When a serial number is found, serial matching is preferred over a typed forklift number because it is safer when source data contains an incorrect unit number. The app shows its proposed match and values for you to review before saving.

The service date should come from Date Completed when the document contains it; otherwise Date Started; if neither can be read, the form falls back to today and you should correct it manually.

The app never needs you to invent hours. If no reliable reading is present, leave Hours blank.

Hour Readings and Current Hours

Forklift Fleet stores the latest Current Hours. Forklift Hour Readings preserves the historical readings. A reading can come from a Manual Update, PM, Repair, or Work Order.

When an accepted new reading differs from Current Hours, the app updates the Fleet record and creates the historical reading. Duplicate guards prevent an identical forklift/date/hours reading from being created twice.

Hours are also an Aging / Grading input, so keeping them current improves replacement-risk visibility.

Primary Service Vendor behavior

Log PM and Log Repair prefill the forklift’s Primary Service Vendor and phone when available. You may use a different vendor for a particular event.

If the service vendor differs from the primary vendor, the app asks whether you want to update the forklift’s Primary Service Vendor. It does not change it silently because a different vendor may be a one-time service provider.

Rentals — open cost, vendor, expense, and follow-up

When a repair requires a rental, turn on Rental Required and enter the Rental Vendor and start date. If the invoice has not arrived, leave cost blank. The rental is saved as Cost Pending and appears in the Open Rentals / Missing Cost follow-up queue.

Once the rental cost is known, the expense belongs in Forklift Transactions with Category = Rental so Rental Spend YTD and Lifetime Rental Spend can be calculated for the affected forklift. Rental Days YTD tracks the duration separately.

Rental expense is part of total forklift operating expense but is currently excluded from Repair Expense YTD used for the Aging / Grading repair-cost score.

PM compliance

PM Count YTD is compared with the forklift’s PM Frequency. Expected annual counts are: Monthly 12, Quarterly 4, Every 4 months 3, Biannual 2, and Annual 1.

The app shows On Track, Behind, or Frequency Missing. PM compliance is currently an operational flag and does not add penalty points to the Aging / Grading score.

Aging / Grading

The grade combines current-year Repair Cost, Service Calls, and Total Hours. PM expense is excluded from Repair Cost for grading, and PMs never count as service calls.

Repair Cost points: $0 = 0; $1–999 = 1; $1,000–1,999 = 2; $2,000–4,999 = 3; $5,000+ = 4.

Service Call points: 0 = 0; 1–2 = 1; 3–4 = 2; 5–6 = 3; more than 6 = 4.

Hours points: under 2,500 = 1; 2,500–5,999 = 2; 6,000–8,999 = 3; 9,000+ = 4.

Grades: 0–3 Excellent; 4–6 Good; 7–9 Watch; 10+ Replace Soon. Click the grade badge to see exactly why a unit received its score.

The formula is intentionally being kept stable while real fleet results are observed. A Watch or Replace Soon grade is a management signal, not an automatic replacement order.

Reports

Aging / Grading can export both Excel and a Leadership PDF. If a ROM filter is selected, the report respects that filter; otherwise it reports the full fleet.

Excel is intended for filtering, auditing, and follow-up. The Leadership PDF is intended as a concise management summary of grades, repair cost, PM compliance, and attention items.

Lease Manager integration and workrooms

Current Workroom choices for operating forklifts are pulled from active Lease Manager locations. This keeps the forklift location list aligned with the property database and reduces spelling/location drift.

Parked units are still owned and physically located somewhere, so they remain tied to a current active workroom unless/until a later transfer or disposition workflow changes that history.

Common warnings and what they mean

Lower Hours warning: the new meter reading is below the existing reading. Continue only if the prior entry was wrong or the meter was replaced/reset.

Primary Vendor mismatch: this service vendor differs from the forklift’s saved primary vendor. Choose whether the difference is temporary or the primary vendor should be updated.

Duplicate / Skip on NetSuite import: a matching transaction already exists. Review it and normally leave it skipped.

Rental Cost Pending: a rental was recorded but its cost/invoice still needs to be entered.

Frequency Missing: PM Frequency is not set for that forklift, so the app cannot judge PM compliance.

Tracking Prevention / favicon 404 in Edge Console: generally browser/privacy or missing tab-icon messages; they do not affect forklift records when the app otherwise loads and saves normally.

What is not finished yet?

#5 Transfers / Status History is the next major module. The current app can store current workroom/status and can change repair-related status, but the dedicated historical workflow for transfers and status changes is still to be designed and added.

The Help & Training section should be updated after #5 so the final transfer, relocation, disposition, and status-history procedures are documented here too.
