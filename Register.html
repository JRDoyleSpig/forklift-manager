Help & Training
How the Forklift Manager works, when to use each workflow, and what data is saved where.
The most important rule: what is the master data?
Forklift Transactions is the financial master record. If money was spent on a forklift, the cost should ultimately exist in Transactions. That is what drives yearly expense, lifetime expense, repair expense, PM spend, rental spend, service-call counts, and Aging / Grading cost metrics.

The other SharePoint lists have different jobs: Forklift Fleet is the current-state master for the asset; Forklift Service Events records the operational story of a PM or repair visit and holds its work orders/attachments; Forklift Hour Readings is the meter-history master; and Forklift Rentals tracks open rentals and costs that may not be known yet. These lists work together rather than replacing Transactions.

When do I use PM Upload vs Open Repair Case?

Upload PM / Work Order / Invoice when planned maintenance occurs and you receive the work order or invoice. That upload becomes the PM service record.

Open Repair Case as soon as a workroom reports a forklift problem—even before a technician visits and long before you know the cost. A repair case stays open through scheduling, diagnosis, quotes, approval, parts, rentals, follow-up visits, and final resolution.

You do not need to create a repair case just because a historical/accounting transaction exists. The practical rule is: Transactions answer “what did we spend?” Repair Cases answer “what is happening with this problem?” PM/Service Events answer “what service actually occurred?”

Very important — avoiding duplicate expenses

If a PM invoice upload creates a confirmed PM transaction, or you enter known expense lines during a repair-case update, those lines are written into Forklift Transactions immediately. If the same invoice later appears in a NetSuite CSV, the import duplicate check should identify matching Date + Forklift ID + Invoice Number + Amount lines and mark them to Skip.

For repairs, you will usually not know the cost yet. That is normal. Open and manage the repair case with no expense amount. When the invoice later posts, let the monthly NetSuite import create the financial transaction. Only enter repair expense manually when you already have a real invoice/known amount and want it recorded immediately.

Always review the NetSuite preview before importing. A changed date, invoice number, forklift assignment, or split amount can prevent an otherwise related line from matching as a duplicate.

Fleet — adding and editing forklifts

The Fleet list represents the forklift’s current state: ID, make/model/serial, current workroom, status, current hours, primary service vendor, PM frequency, attachments/poles, acquisition information, estimated replacement cost, and notes.

Estimated Replacement Cost is entered manually for each forklift and is used only for lifetime-spend context/flagging. It does not directly add grade points.

Double-click a forklift to edit it. Current workrooms are validated against active Lease Manager locations for Active, Down for Repair, and Parked units. Retired/Scrapped/Sold units may remain associated with an inactive historical location.

Changing Current Hours automatically creates a reading in Forklift Hour Readings. If the new reading is lower than the current reading, the app warns you and requires confirmation.

Status definitions

Active: In normal operating service.

Down for Repair: Temporarily unavailable because it needs or is receiving repair. It is expected to return to service.

Parked: Still owned and physically retained, but intentionally not being used or repaired at this time. It may return to service later. Parked units remain visible but are excluded from the Active count.

Retired: Permanently removed from normal operating service with no intention to return it to regular use. It may still be physically on site while disposition is decided.

Sold: Ownership has been transferred through sale.

Scrapped: Permanently disposed of as scrap.

Disposition Date is manually entered for Retired, Sold, and Scrapped. The app does not automatically invent a disposition date.

Transactions — financial master data

Transactions contains the expense lines used for financial reporting and grading. Negative transactions/credits reduce totals. The app calculates yearly expense and lifetime expense live from the transaction history rather than storing fixed totals on the forklift.

Typical categories include PM Service, Repair / Expense, Rental, and other expense classifications. PM expense counts toward total operating expense but is excluded from Repair Expense YTD used in the Aging / Grading repair score. Rental expense also counts toward total expense but is currently kept separate from grading repair cost.

Double-click a transaction to correct fields such as forklift, workroom, vendor, invoice, amount, category, service-call flag, hours, downtime, rental required, description, URL, or notes.

Major Life-Extending Repair: for a repair that materially restores a major component and is expected to extend useful life, double-click the repair transaction and manually check Major Life-Extending Repair. Major Repair Notes are required. The designation reduces only the grading contribution; it does not reduce the actual financial spend shown in history.

NetSuite CSV import

Use the monthly NetSuite CSV for ongoing accounting expenses. The importer reads the forklift from the Memo, vendor from Main Line Name, and presents a review screen before anything is saved.

PMs are suggested using the known PM patterns (including the common $135 PM labor charge), but the category remains editable. Repair invoices can receive one high-confidence Service Call suggestion. PMs never count as service calls.

Primary Service Vendor is compared during review. A different vendor does not automatically replace the primary vendor because one-off vendors are common.

What exactly counts as a Service Call?

A service call represents the visit/event, not every invoice line. A multi-line repair invoice may contain labor, parts, freight, and other charges, but only one line should carry Service Call = Yes for that repair event.

PMs always have Service Call = No. Rental-only expense lines also do not count as service calls. This prevents service-call counts from being inflated by accounting line detail.

PM workflow — work orders and invoices

Upload PM / Work Order / Invoice is the normal PM workflow. Upload the PM work order when you receive it, or upload the PM invoice if that is the document you have.

The app detects PM wording, matches the forklift by serial when possible, saves the document as a PM Service Event, updates Last PM Date, and records Current Hours / Hour Readings when a meter reading is present.

If the uploaded document is an invoice and you confirm an invoice amount, the app also creates a PM Service transaction immediately with Service Call = No. If there is no known cost yet, leave Invoice Amount blank; the later monthly NetSuite import will create the financial transaction.

PM expense is excluded from repair-cost grading and PMs never count as service calls.

Repair Cases — the full workflow

1. Open the case when the workroom reports the problem. Enter the forklift, date reported, problem/request, who reported it if useful, vendor, current case status, and Forklift Availability. For initial service, choose Not Scheduled, Requested / Awaiting Date, or Scheduled — Date Confirmed. Only enter an Initial Service Date when the vendor actually gives you one. No cost is required.

2. Manage the same case as things change. Double-click it in Open Repair Cases and add an update. Typical statuses are New Request, Service Scheduled, Diagnosing, Waiting on Quote, Waiting on Approval, Waiting on Parts, Follow-Up Service Needed, Replacement Recommended, Repaired / Resolved, Parked, Retired / Disposed, or Cancelled / No Longer Needed. Rental status stays separate from repair-case status.

3. Availability and Fleet Status are separate from rental. A lift may be Usable, Limited Use, or Down / Unusable while the repair is open. Down / Unusable suggests Fleet Status = Down for Repair, but it does not automatically require a rental. A rental can be added later if downtime becomes long enough to justify one.

4. Add every useful technician visit/work order to that case. An update can store the vendor, work-order/invoice reference, hours, downtime, attachments, notes, rental information, and an optional Fleet status change. Hours update Current Hours and Hour Readings only after confirmation.

5. Costs normally come later. You usually leave repair expense blank and allow the monthly NetSuite import to create the Transactions. If you already have a final invoice and enter expense lines during the update, they become Transactions immediately and duplicate safety should skip them during the later CSV import.

6. Close the case only for a final outcome. Repaired / Resolved, Parked, Retired / Disposed, or Cancelled / No Longer Needed remove the case from the Open Repair Cases queue. Replacement Recommended stays open until a final decision is made.

Attachments: photos, quotes, work orders, and invoices saved with a Repair Case update appear in Case History. Click an attachment filename to open the SharePoint file and confirm what was saved with that update.

PM / Work Order / Invoice upload

This workflow is for PM work orders, PM invoices, and other service documents. It may contain an hour-meter reading and/or invoice cost, but neither is required. The app reads text from the uploaded PDF and looks for serial number, Hours, Work Order number, Date Completed (preferred), Date Started, vendor indicators, and PM wording.

When a serial number is found, serial matching is preferred over a typed forklift number because it is safer when source data contains an incorrect unit number. The app shows its proposed match and values for you to review before saving.

The service date should come from Date Completed when the document contains it; otherwise Date Started; if neither can be read, the form falls back to today and you should correct it manually.

The app never needs you to invent hours or cost. If no reliable reading or invoice amount is present, leave that field blank.

Hour Readings and Current Hours

Forklift Fleet stores the latest Current Hours. Forklift Hour Readings preserves the historical readings. A reading can come from a Manual Update, PM, Repair, or Work Order.

When an accepted new reading differs from Current Hours, the app updates the Fleet record and creates the historical reading. Duplicate guards prevent an identical forklift/date/hours reading from being created twice.

Hours are also an Aging / Grading input, so keeping them current improves replacement-risk visibility.

Primary Service Vendor behavior

PM uploads, Open Repair Case, and repair updates prefill the forklift’s Primary Service Vendor and phone when available. You may use a different vendor for a particular event.

If the service vendor differs from the primary vendor, the app asks whether you want to update the forklift’s Primary Service Vendor. It does not change it silently because a different vendor may be a one-time service provider.

Rentals — status, cost, invoices, and transactions

Rental need is separate from forklift availability and repair-case status. Use Rental Needed / Approved only after the decision is made to obtain a rental.

Rental Status tracks Requested, Active, Returned, or Cancelled. Cost Status separately tracks Pending or Complete.

Double-click a rental in Open Rentals / Missing Cost to manage it. When the final cost is entered and Cost Status becomes Complete, the app creates a Rental transaction unless a matching rental transaction already exists. You may also upload the rental invoice there; the app proposes vendor, reference, and amount and warns you if it cannot confidently determine key information.

The later monthly NetSuite import still checks for duplicates. Always review the import preview before saving.

Rentals — open cost, vendor, expense, and follow-up

A repair case does not automatically require a rental. Track whether the forklift is Usable, Limited Use, or Down / Unusable first. If a down unit remains unavailable long enough that a rental is justified, add a repair-case update with Rental Needed / Approved. Rental status is tracked separately as Requested, Active, Returned, or Cancelled. Cost status is separately Pending or Complete. If the invoice has not arrived, leave cost blank and Cost Status = Pending; it remains in Open Rentals / Missing Cost until completed.

Once the rental cost is known, the expense belongs in Forklift Transactions with Category = Rental so Rental Spend YTD and Lifetime Rental Spend can be calculated for the affected forklift. Rental Days YTD tracks the duration separately.

Rental expense is part of total forklift operating expense but is currently excluded from Repair Expense YTD used for the Aging / Grading repair-cost score.

PM compliance

PM Count YTD is compared with the forklift’s PM Frequency. Expected annual counts are: Monthly 12, Quarterly 4, Every 4 months 3, Biannual 2, and Annual 1.

The app shows On Track, Behind, or Frequency Missing. PM compliance is currently an operational flag and does not add penalty points to the Aging / Grading score.

Aging / Grading

The grade combines weighted recent Repair Cost, weighted Service Calls, and Total Hours. PM expense and rental expense are excluded from repair-cost grading. PMs and rental-only transactions never count as service calls.

Repair history weighting: current year = 100%; prior year = 50%; two years prior = 25%. Older repair history remains visible in annual and lifetime reporting but does not add repair points.

Major Life-Extending Repairs: these are manually identified on a repair transaction when the work materially restores a major component and is expected to extend useful life. Major Repair Notes are required. The full actual cost remains in annual and lifetime spend, but only 50% of the repair cost is used for grading before the year weighting is applied. The app never assigns this designation automatically based on dollar amount.

Weighted Repair Cost points: $0 = 0; $1–$1,499 = 1; $1,500–$2,999 = 2; $3,000–$5,999 = 3; $6,000+ = 4.

Service-call history weighting: current year = 100%; prior year = 75%; two years prior = 50%; three years prior = 10%. Older calls remain informational only.

Weighted Service Call points: under 1.00 = 0; 1.00–2.99 = 1; 3.00–4.99 = 2; 5.00–6.99 = 3; 7.00+ = 4.

Hours points: under 2,500 = 0; 2,500–5,999 = 1; 6,000–8,999 = 2; 9,000–11,999 = 3; 12,000+ = 4.

Grades: 0–3 Excellent; 4–6 Good; 7–8 Watch; 9+ Replace Soon. Click the grade badge to see the score breakdown. Replace Soon is a management signal, not an automatic replacement order.

Lifetime Repair Spend does not add grade points. It is management context. When Estimated Replacement Cost is entered for the forklift, lifetime repair spend is compared with it: under 50% = no flag; 50–99% = Elevated Lifetime Spend; 100%+ = High Lifetime Spend.

The Aging / Grading screen deliberately shows both Current-Year Actual Repair Spend and the Weighted Repair Amount used for grading so the real financial history is never confused with the scoring calculation.

Reports

Aging / Grading can export both Excel and a Leadership PDF. If a ROM filter is selected, the report respects that filter; otherwise it reports the full grading fleet.

Excel is the detailed audit report. It includes current-year actual repair spend, weighted repair amount, weighted service calls, lifetime repair spend, estimated replacement cost and lifetime-spend flag, Major Life-Extending Repair totals, point components, grade, and annual history by year including each year's weighted contribution.

Leadership PDF is the concise management view. It highlights Watch / Replace Soon units and shows the actual-versus-weighted repair context, weighted calls, lifetime repair spend, life-extending repair indicator, replacement-cost context, score, and grade.

Lease Manager integration and workrooms

Current Workroom choices for operating forklifts are pulled from active Lease Manager locations. This keeps the forklift location list aligned with the property database and reduces spelling/location drift.

Parked units are still owned and physically located somewhere, so they remain tied to a current active workroom unless/until a later transfer or disposition workflow changes that history.

Common warnings and what they mean

Lower Hours warning: the new meter reading is below the existing reading. Continue only if the prior entry was wrong or the meter was replaced/reset.

Primary Vendor mismatch: this service vendor differs from the forklift’s saved primary vendor. Choose whether the difference is temporary or the primary vendor should be updated.

Duplicate / Skip on NetSuite import: a matching transaction already exists. Review it and normally leave it skipped.

Rental Cost Pending: a rental was recorded but its cost/invoice still needs to be entered.

Frequency Missing: PM Frequency is not set for that forklift, so the app cannot judge PM compliance.

Tracking Prevention / favicon 404 in Edge Console: generally browser/privacy or missing tab-icon messages; they do not affect forklift records when the app otherwise loads and saves normally.

Forklift Budget — 6022 repair/maintenance and 6023 rental

Forklift Budget is a forecasting and budget-control view. It tracks only 6022 Forklift Repair & Maintenance and 6023 Forklift Rental. Fuel, poles, and baler expense are intentionally excluded.

Monthly budgets never carry forward, and 6022 and 6023 are separate budget pools. Repair capacity is judged only against that month’s 6022 Repair & Maintenance budget; rental capacity is judged only against that month’s 6023 Rental budget. Unused rental budget is never shown as money available for additional repairs, and an under-budget prior month never increases the next month’s allowance.

Accounting Actual comes from Forklift Transactions using the transaction/accounting month. Service date and accounting month are deliberately separate because an invoice may post after the work was performed.

Projected Exposure is calculated separately by account. 6022 combines repair/PM actuals with open-repair reserves and expected PMs. 6023 combines rental actuals with rental forecasts. Each account has its own Normal below 75%, Watch at 75–89%, High Risk at 90–99%, and Over Budget at 100%+ warning level.

Unknown repair reserves are automatic. The baseline is calculated from historical non-PM repair events using the 75th percentile rather than requiring you to guess a repair cost. Problem/diagnosis text, issue type, case status, timing in the month, and known quote information can revise the forecast as better information becomes available.

Expected Expense Month is automatic. The app uses repair status, timing, service/quote progress, and expected billing lag to place exposure into the month where the invoice is most likely to hit accounting. Near-month-end repairs commonly forecast into the following month. Manual overrides are available from the Budget detail table and require a reason.

PM forecasts are separate from repair reserves. The app uses historical PM invoice groups, observed PM spacing, known PM fields/frequency when available, and historical PM cost. A forecast does not create a Transaction; the actual expense replaces the forecast when it posts.

Rental forecasts use known daily/weekly/monthly rates first and historical rental cost as a fallback. Multi-month rentals are spread across the expected accounting months rather than charged entirely to the rental start month.

Forklift Budget — annual upload, historical review, and reports

Upload Annual Budget reads only 6022 and 6023 from the workbook. The app shows a preview before saving the 24 monthly records. Re-uploading a corrected year updates the same Year + Month + Account records instead of intentionally creating duplicates.

If a new year begins before its official budget is uploaded, the app temporarily reuses the matching prior-year monthly budget and clearly labels it as a fallback. Official SharePoint budget records are not fabricated for the missing year.

Use Prior Month / Next Month to review historical months. Current month reports are marked In Progress and include actual-to-date plus projected exposure. When a month is closed, the view switches to final actuals, budget variance, final forecast-at-close, and forecast accuracy. Late transactions remain allowed but trigger a Changed After Close flag. Future months are forecasting views only.

Leadership PDF is the concise management view. Excel Detail contains the item-level drivers. Forecast changes are stored in Forklift Budget Forecast History so the estimate → quote/known cost → actual progression can be audited and the forecasting model can improve over time.
